# Video demo script (about 9 to 10 minutes)

The brief asks for 5 to 10 minutes that **demonstrate the functions**, with little research background. This script spends under a minute on context and the rest on the running app, in the order of the proposal's requirements (FR2 to FR10).

**Setup before recording**

* Phone charged, brightness up, Do Not Disturb on.
* Log in as the parent **before** recording, and keep the app open: it asks for the login code each time it is opened.
* Progress reset (Grown-ups > Settings > Delete progress on this phone) so the demo starts fresh, or a second phone with every lesson finished, to jump straight to any lesson.
* Phone mirrored to the laptop with **scrcpy**, recorded in **OBS** with the mirrored phone and your webcam.
* Microphone check, for you and for the voice coach. Speak slowly; you can re-record any section.

---

| Time | What is on screen | What to say (say it in your own words) |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00 – 0:30** | Title slide or the app's splash screen | "Hi, I'm Audrey Munezero. SafeTouch is an Android app with a 3D guide character, Keza, that teaches children aged 6 to 11 in Rwanda to recognise unsafe situations and get help from a trusted adult." |
| **0:30 – 1:10** | The Unity editor with the project open | Show the Unity version (Unity 6 LTS) and the **SafeTouch** menu. In the Hierarchy, show **SafeTouch App** with GameManager, ARManager, AudioManager, ScenarioManager, ProgressManager. Say: "These are the classes from my class diagram. Keza lives in her own 3D world, so any Android 8 phone runs it without a camera. Lesson content is JSON, so experts can correct wording without code." |
| **1:10 – 1:40** | Mirrored phone: menu, language switch, lesson map | Switch English and Kinyarwanda once (FR10), then back. On the lesson map, show the Explorer (6-8) and Guardian (9-11) paths. |
| **1:40 – 3:00** | **Lesson 1, My Space Bubble** (FR2, FR3) | Keza appears at once in her park. Drag her, pinch to zoom, swipe to look around. Tap each zone: red 0.3 m, yellow 0.6 m, green 0.9 m. Let Grandma and the classmate walk in, then the stranger: first choose **Let them come closer** to show the gentle "Let's think again" (FR5), then **Say "Stop!"**. |
| **3:00 – 4:30** | **Lesson 2, Safe or Unsafe?** (FR4, FR5) | Play one card as a 3D story. Answer once wrongly to show the explanation, then rightly. Show the act-out: the child takes Keza to Mum. |
| **4:30 – 5:40** | **Lesson 3, STOP!** (FR6, voice coach) | Collect S-T-O-P around Keza. On **S**, Keza's card asks to use the microphone; tap **Yes**, then Android's **Allow**. Say "NO!" out loud: the NO power meter rises, with stars and the loud / steady / short checks. |
| **5:40 – 6:40** | **Lesson 4, My Trusted Adults** (FR7) | Pick four or five roles (Mum or Dad, Teacher, IZU volunteer, Nurse or doctor); they stand around Keza. Choose who Keza tells first. |
| **6:40 – 7:30** | **My badges** (FR8) and **My NO** | Show the rank card, the journey path with stars, the badge shelf (tap a locked badge to see how to win it). Open **My NO**: play back the NO you just said, show the growth chart and the goals. |
| **7:30 – 8:30** | **Grown-ups** (FR9) | The grown-up check. **My child**: the trusted adults guess and the child's NOs. **Talk**, **Signs** and **Get help** (Child Helpline 116, Isange One Stop Centre). **Settings**: anonymous sync off by default; keeping NO recordings can be switched off. |
| **8:30 – 9:10** | *(Optional)* **Memory Quest** or **Bubble Guard** | One round of either: the Memory Quest chase, or a Bubble Guard visitor. Say: "The games bring the lessons back, so the child recalls each idea instead of re-reading it." |
| **9:10 – 9:40** | The README | "The code, tests, designs and deployment plan are in the repository. Next: the expert review of the content and the pilot with children in Kigali. Thank you." |

**If something fails while recording**, stop and re-record that section only; you can join the clips in OBS or any editor.

**Upload:** YouTube (Unlisted) or Google Drive (anyone with the link can view). Paste the link into the README.
